**Date**: November 29, 2025  
**Asset**: SynPhytica Cannabis Knowledge Graph (SCKG)  
**Objective**: Leverage proprietary data assets to secure funding, partnerships, and revenue.

---

## 💎 The Asset: SynPhytica Cannabis Knowledge Graph (SCKG)

The SCKG is not merely a database; it is a structured, AI-ready ontology connecting the biological, chemical, and pharmacological dimensions of *Cannabis sativa*.

### Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
1.  **Standardization**: Solves the "messy data" problem in cannabis research with a rigorous schema.
2.  **AI-Readiness**: Formatted specifically for Machine Learning (Transformers/GNNs), unlike legacy databases.
3.  **Multi-Dimensionality**: Links genetics (strains) -> chemistry (compounds) -> pharmacology (targets) -> clinical outcomes.

## 🏛️ Business Models

### 1. The "Data Grant" Program (Academic Partnerships)
**Target**: Universities & Research Institutes (USP, UNIFESP, Technion Israel, UC Davis)

*   **Concept**: Instead of charging cash, we "grant" access to the Pro Tier of SCKG in exchange for structured data contribution.
*   **Mechanism**:
    *   Researcher gets: Access to our AI tools + Curated Database.
    *   Researcher gives: Their raw experimental data formatted to our Schema.
*   **Benefit**: Rapidly scales the database with high-quality, peer-reviewed data at zero acquisition cost.

### 2. Commercial Licensing (Pharma & AgTech)
**Target**: Licensed Producers (LPs), Pharma Companies, Testing Labs

*   **Tier A (Discovery License)**:
    *   Access to chemical-pharmacological mappings.
    *   Use case: R&D of new formulations.
    *   **Price**: $25k - $50k / year.
*   **Tier B (Full Enterprise)**:
    *   Includes genetic data and patient outcome correlations.
    *   Use case: Clinical trial design, precision breeding.
    *   **Price**: $100k+ / year + Royalties on developed IP.

### 3. Foundation Grants & Public Funding
**Target**: NIH, FAPESP, Chan Zuckerberg Initiative

*   **Narrative**: "SynPhytica is building the 'Human Genome Project' of Phytomedicine."
*   **Ask**: Funding to build the infrastructure for open science (while keeping high-value aggregations proprietary).
*   **Goal**: $250k - $1M in non-dilutive funding.

## 🗺️ Roadmap to Monetization

### Phase 1: Packaging (Months 1-3)
*   [ ] **Clean & Validate**: Ensure the current proprietary dataset (14+ compounds) is flawless.
*   [ ] **Enrich**: Use the automated scripts to add metadata (molecular weights, targets) to all entries.
*   [ ] **White Paper**: Publish a "Data Descriptor" paper explaining the methodology and schema (without releasing the full data).

### Phase 2: The "Pilot Partner" (Months 4-6)
*   [ ] **Select 1 Academic Partner**: A university lab studying cannabis.
*   [ ] **Offer**: Free access to SynPhytica's AI in exchange for beta-testing the database schema.
*   [ ] **Goal**: Validate that external researchers *can* and *want* to use our standard.

### Phase 3: Launch Data Grant Program (Months 7-12)
*   [ ] **Open Call**: Announce the "SynPhytica Data Grant".
*   [ ] **Onboard**: Select 5-10 research groups.
*   [ ] **Network Effect**: As the database grows, its value grows exponentially, attracting paying commercial clients.

## 🛡️ Data Governance & Security

To monetize data, trust is paramount.

1.  **The "Vault" Architecture**:
    *   **Public Layer**: Metadata, Schema, Templates (Open Source).
    *   **Community Layer**: Data contributed by partners (Shared Access).
    *   **Proprietary Layer**: SynPhytica's core curated data + Commercial insights (Closed).

2.  **Traceability**:
    *   Every data point is tagged with its source (Researcher/Institution).
    *   Blockchain/Ledger (future) to track data usage and attribute royalties.
